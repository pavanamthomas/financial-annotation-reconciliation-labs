# Roadmap

Bank-file, sampling, and opinion claims this laboratory still does not
make (August 2026).

## In scope now

- Constructed cash recs, unexplained difference, stale outstanding
  checks, completeness/existence, cutoff, classification, three-way
  quantity and amount.
- A clean rec on which error-level checks stay quiet.

## Remaining bounds

1. No bank-file parser.
2. No multi-currency translation.
3. No sampling inference about control failure rates.

## Explicitly not in scope

- An audit opinion or SOX conclusion.
- Treating a green test suite as evidence about a live rec pack.

Close an issue only with a test, a note in `CASE_STUDY.md`, or an
explicit limitation.
